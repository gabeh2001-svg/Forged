Label reader files for Swell.
tesseract-core-*.wasm.js: Tesseract OCR compiled to WebAssembly, from tesseract.js-core v5.1.1 (Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core).
eng.traineddata: English model from tessdata_fast (Apache License 2.0, https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast).
worker.js: Swell's own wrapper. Runs on the phone; photos are never uploaded.
